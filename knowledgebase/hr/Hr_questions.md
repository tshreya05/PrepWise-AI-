6. Why do you want to work here?

Career & Choices

16. What would you do if you didn't get this job?

Work Style & Values
24. How do you handle stress or pressure?

26. How do you handle criticism?
27. What motivates you at work?
28. Describe your ideal work environment.
29. How do you define success?

Situational / Judgment
31. If you saw a colleague doing something unethical, what would you do?
32. How would you handle being asked to do something outside your job description?
33. What would you do if you strongly disagreed with a company policy?
34. How do you prioritize when your manager and a client have conflicting demands?

Closing
